# Project brand sources

Verified on October 2, 2026. Logos and published illustrations belong to their respective brands. The portfolio frames, lighting, layout, and interaction are original compositions. Product screenshots illustrate team projects and do not imply sole authorship of the products or their design.

## Bask Health

- [Official site](https://bask.health).
- `public/projects/bask-logo.svg`: [official SVG](https://bask.health/landing-pages/_next/static/media/bask-logo.d20a50e7.svg). Original logo fills: `#8CC5FA`, `#1A59BF`.
- `public/projects/bask-house.webp`: [public modular telehealth illustration](https://bask.health/landing-pages/main-house-video-poster.png), 3027 × 1188. Exported from the original transparent PNG as WebP at quality 85 for local delivery, with its dimensions and transparency preserved.
- [Landing CSS](https://bask.health/landing-pages/_next/static/css/6fc9fe1d7dd5701e.css): dark surface `#080808`, navy glow `#0C2959`, light surface `#FAFAFA`. The portfolio uses a navy stage derived from these colors.
